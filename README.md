# OrderLimit EA for MetaTrader 4 (MT4)

OrderLimit EA is a specialized Expert Advisor designed for the MetaTrader 4 platform to assist traders with automated order management and position limits.

---

## ⚡ Setup & Critical Requirements

To ensure the EA functions correctly and can execute trades on your account, you **must** configure the following settings after attaching it to your chart:

1. **Allow Live Trading**: When adding the EA to your chart, navigate to the **Common** tab and check the **"Allow live trading"** box.
2. **Activate Automated Trading**: Click the **Auto Trading** button in the top toolbar of MetaTrader 4.
3. **Verify the Status**: Look at the **smiley face icon** in the top right corner of your chart window.
    * 😊 **Smiling Face**: The EA is active and working correctly.
    * 🙁 **Sad Face**: Live trading is blocked. Double-check your settings.
